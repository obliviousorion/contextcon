# Ethics review — #18 LaneBook — 2026-11-22 15:43

| Idea | Score | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| #18 LaneBook | **2** | Markets on real carriers' distress proxies; framing risk unresolved |

## Top 3 concerns

1. **Unresolved framing risk, by the idea's own admission (§14 risk 1).** A "prediction market"/event-contract/desk/stake/price vocabulary applied to real, named carriers' operational fate reads as an internal betting pool on partners' misfortune, regardless of sandboxed money or single-tenant scope. Gambling and defamation optics come from the *framing*, not from who can see the ledger — "no outside traders" doesn't neutralize the optics of staff wagering on whether a named vendor's headcount collapses.
2. **Still acting on proxies, just relabeled.** Settlement criteria (headcount below a threshold, delisting, a filing field) are the same proxy-for-distress signals the council rejected in Sentinel. §9's claim that "the price is a guess, the settlement never is" is a testability argument, not an ethics one: a YES settlement on "headcount fell below 150" *is* a dated negative finding about a real company, produced and acted on (payout) inside the 3PL — exactly the harm vector Sentinel was marked down for, just wrapped in a falsifiable criterion.
3. **Right of reply is reactive, not preventive.** §9 lets a carrier "request the full audit trail and dispute the source record" only after settlement/payout approval has already fired. Sentinel's design (which this idea claims to improve on) holds any negative action *pending* the counterparty's reply before anything happens. LaneBook demotes that to an after-the-fact appeal — a regression on the exact axis (harm from false positives) this lens scores.

## Fatal flaws
Not an automatic fail — harmful steps (trade above cap, payout) are gated by human approval, and no person-level data is used. But the combination of (a) an explicitly self-flagged, unresolved gambling/defamation framing risk and (b) settlement that still functions as a proxy-based negative verdict on a real counterparty makes this un-buildable as specified without a redesign; it should not go to Round 1 in its current form.

## Recommended merges/changes
- Strip all market/trading vocabulary (trade, stake, desk, price) before this is scored again — keep only the forward-dated attestation mechanism and frame it as a scheduled fact-check, not a bet.
- Add a pre-settlement hold-for-reply gate (mirroring Sentinel's "held reply before action") so a negative settlement cannot release payout before the named carrier has had a chance to contest the source record.
- Restrict allowed criterion types to operational/contractual facts (capacity, SLA, delivery dates) and exclude solvency-adjacent proxies (headcount decline, default-adjacent thresholds) to avoid the proxy-for-distress pattern entirely.
- Consider merging the attestation engine directly into AsOf as a "forward-dated attest" feature rather than shipping it as a separate market product — the idea itself concedes (§14 risk 3) it is "AsOf's engine... with a sportsbook skin."
