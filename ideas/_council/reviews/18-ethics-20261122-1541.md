# Ethics review: #18 Benefits Desk (score 2/5)

| Idea | Score | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| #18 Benefits Desk | 2 | Profiles named employees; job-change watch; right of reply is thin |

**Why 2, not lower:** opt-in, stated purpose, Told/Checked/Guessed labels, no guessing age/health/family, cited insurer figures, buying gated by the person.

**Why not 3+:**
- It enriches and watches individuals (person enrich, job-change watcher) and is not company-only.
- The same watcher design got #14 dropped as surveillance. Opt-in helps, but the opt-in is "profile link" and the employer is the client. Consent could be coerced or implied in an employment setting.
- Deletion is promised ("deleted afterwards") but has no mechanism or retention period.
- "Who could be harmed" is UNKNOWN.
- Inferring a job change from a record can be wrong (stale, lookalike name, wrong person). A false alarm gets the person to convert cover. It is gated, but still real harm.
- Regulated activity (insurance advice), and the commission-blind ranking is unverified.
- No duplicate or lookalike case, so wrong-person risk is untested.

**Top 3 (of this single idea set):** only #18 reviewed; its safest slice is the employer-side benchmark (company and job-post data only).

**Fatal flaws:**
- Person-level watcher plus employer-as-client optics (workforce surveillance).
- Identity resolution for a person with no consent record is undefined; wrong-person match leaks data.

**Recommended merges / cuts:**
- Cut the cover-gap alarm and person enrich; make the employee flow self-declared (Told only), no Crustdata person data.
- Keep the benchmark and insurer shortlist (company data, cited).
- Add a named delete step, retention period, and a lookalike-person refusal case ("cannot verify").
