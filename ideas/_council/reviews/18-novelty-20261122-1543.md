# Novelty and story review — #18 LaneBook (2026-11-22 15:43)

| Idea | Score | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| #18 LaneBook | **3/5** | Forward-settlement twist is fresh, but engine, demo beats, even phrasing lifted from AsOf |

## Top-3 concerns
1. **It is AsOf wearing a market skin.** §13 says it outright: "AsOf's engine, pointed at a forward-dated criterion." The identify/resolve/attest/outbox/idempotency machinery, the "Shree Balaji → 25 candidates" beat, the kill-mid-action-restart beat, and the per-tenant key design are all copy-pasted demo content, not new engineering. A judge who just watched AsOf's slot will recognize the architecture inside the first 30 seconds.
2. **The ethics framing is a live grenade, not a mitigation.** The doc's own §14.1 admits a prediction-market mechanic — even sandboxed, single-tenant, no outside traders — "may read to the ethics judge as gambling/market-making." With ethics worth 25 of 100 points and no resolved reframing, this is a self-inflicted risk a reviewer shouldn't have to flag for them.
3. **Thin incremental payoff for the novelty it claims.** The actual new surface area is: a pricing layer (LMSR, cut-first per §13) and a label ("aggregated estimate"). Everything else — refusal-first settlement, EXTENDED/CANNOT VERIFY, duplicate dedupe, criterion validator — is AsOf's `attest()` logic restated for a close_date instead of an as-of date. That's a feature add to AsOf, argued as a sibling idea.

## Fatal flaws
None outright disqualifying under RULES §1 (serves the one client, sourced facts, human approval present), but the gambling-framing risk is close to a §1-adjacent "ethics judge kills it on sight" problem if not resolved before Round 1, and the uniqueness claim ("other teams won't build yours") is weak when the strongest argument for uniqueness is "we already built 90% of it for a different idea."

## Suggested reframing
Drop "event-contract desk" and "Kalshi-shaped" language entirely — it invites the gambling read for free. Reframe as **"Forward AsOf": a pre-committed, falsifiable prediction register** — ops states today, in writing, exactly what dated record would prove it wrong, before the fact is known. No staking, no trading, no price, no payout: just a public (internal) ledger of "we said X would be true by date D, citing record type R" that auto-grades itself against Crustdata when D arrives. This keeps the one genuinely new idea (resolution criteria fixed *before* the event, not after) and removes every element a judge could call gambling. It also fixes the demo: the memorable beat becomes "procurement predicted carrier X would stay above 200 headcount; watch this get graded live, YES/NO/EXTENDED, with zero room for the prediction to be fudged after the fact" — a forecasting-accountability story, not a betting one.

## Recommended merges/changes
- If the team wants this on the board, merge it as a **named feature/mode of AsOf** ("AsOf: Forward Mode"), not a standalone idea — matches how Sentinel/FactGate/Golden Record were absorbed rather than kept as siblings.
- Cut the trading/stake/payout mechanics (§4 `trade`, §6 LMSR, §8 payout approval) before Round 1; they add ethics surface area and finance-estimate weakness (no sourced rate card, per §14.2) without adding to the core novelty.
- Keep: criterion-validator-at-creation (genuinely stricter than AsOf's attest-time refusal), EXTENDED state, and the forward/backward framing line — that's the real contribution worth keeping.
