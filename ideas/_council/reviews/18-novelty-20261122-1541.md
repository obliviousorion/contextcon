# Novelty and story review: #18 Benefits Desk (score 3/5)

| Idea | Score | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| #18 Benefits Desk | 3 | Employee-side insurance is fresh; job-post benchmark is unsourced, unproven |

## Assessment
- **Copy risk:** low. Track examples are underwriting a business, hiring, vendor checks. Insurance advice for the client's own staff is a different angle. Risk: a team on 4.1 Underwriting Desk plus "benefits" may blur it.
- **Surprise for a Crustdata engineer / YC partner:** moderate. Job posts as a "benefits market signal" is clever, but 113 of 3,362 posts mentioning insurance is a thin, unrecorded number. Job-change watcher is the same pattern as dropped #14 (surveillance).
- **Demo moment:** none written. Best candidate: a driver changes employer, the alarm fires with the date group cover ends, and a prepared conversion request waits for approval. Kill on stage mid-application, restart, one request only.
- **People's Choice:** plausible. Drivers and delivery partners are relatable; "your cover ends in 30 days" is human. Heavy scope (8 request types) dilutes it.

## Top 3
Only one idea in this file: Benefits Desk, best as the cover-gap alarm alone.

## Fatal flaws
- No demo script, no build plan, no "why others won't build it" (all UNKNOWN).
- Core data (job search, person watch) unverified; our own job/search call returned 400.
- Licence: comparing insurance is regulated in India; this is a broker tool, buyer unknown.
- Overlap with dropped #14 invites an ethics objection.

## Reframing and merges
- Reframe: "The cover that ends the day you quit." One hero flow: opt-in driver, job change detected, gap alarm, approval-gated conversion request. Drop benchmark, insurer ranking and variants to a slide.
- Merge: take the opt-in watcher design from #14 and the Told/Checked/Guessed labels as the facts story. Do not merge the shipment cover variant.
