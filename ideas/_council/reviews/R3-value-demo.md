## Best buyer framing (3PL optional)
Top pick: Second Opinion's graded diff, run on AsOf's dated-attestation engine. Buyer: the diligence or credit-risk team at a bank, NBFC or fund that already pays a KYB or diligence firm. They want an independent check of that firm's pack. Pitch: "We re-check every line of the report you paid for, as of the date you relied on it." Second buyer: an insurer or arbitrator who needs "what was knowable on date D".

## 2-minute demo outline (Second Opinion on AsOf)
1. 0:00-0:20. Hand over a vendor's 10-line carrier pack in plain words: "Check what we paid for."
2. 0:20-1:00. The diff fills in live. Delhivery "30k staff" is CONTRADICT (35,295 on 2026-11-16). The two Delhivery lines resolve to id 5798, so they get one verdict and one charge. "Shree Balaji" is CANNOT VERIFY with 25 candidates shown.
3. 1:00-1:30. Memorable moment: kill the process mid-run. It restarts and finishes with no double charge. A Crustdata watcher alert then marks one line STALE.
4. 1:30-2:00. Approval gate before the contradiction report is sent to the sandbox webhook. Close on the ledger (about 5 credits per pack) and the hardest case: the "4x growth" claim is reported as two dated values side by side, never as a ratio of two lower bounds.

Caveat: a judge may say "this is a vendor check". Open with the job of auditing the auditor.
